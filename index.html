📚 Bioreactor Scientific Principles & Formulas

▼

Modified Gompertz Batch kinetics

Describes batch Biochemical Methane Potential (BMP) cumulative curves:

y(t) = P * exp(-exp( (Rm * e / P) * (λ - t) + 1 ))

Where P is maximum yield potential, Rm is max biogas rate, and λ is the initial lag phase. Enhancements like thermal hydrolysis reduce lag time and open cellular matrices, boosting active rates.

Continuous 4-State Reactor ODE System

Models continuous mass balances in a completely mixed reactor (CSTR):

dS/dt = D*(S_in - S) - kh * S * X_a
dSVFA/dt = D*(SVFA_in - SVFA) + Y_VFA * kh * S * X_a - (1/Y_m) * μ_m(SVFA, pH) * X_m

pH Calculation: Bicarbonate Alkalinity neutralizes VFAs. Excess VFA levels destroy buffer capacity, dropping pH and slowing methanogen archaea growth, causing reactor failure.

Enhancement Mechanisms

Thermal Hydrolysis: Breaks down complex lignin/hemicellulose barriers, increasing the hydrolysis rate constant ($k_h$) by up to 2.5x.

Biochar Dosing: Serves as a conductive carbon backbone. Accelerates Direct Interspecies Electron Transfer (DIET) between syntrophic bacteria and methanogens, shielding archaea from volatile acid inhibition.
